What is GIGO?

Garbage in, garbage out. A model is only as good as its training data. Missing, loose or wrong labels become missing, late or wrong decisions.

How scoring works

Boxes

Fit (IoU) says how well your box matches the object: 100% is exact.

< 30%Not seen
30–75%Seen late
75%+Seen reliably
80%+Full points

Wrong class: counted, but misread. Duplicate box: −3. Box on nothing: −4.

Text

Each right label earns 10 points. Wrong or missing labels earn none.

Glossary

Annotation
A label on data: a box, a class or a judgement.
Ground truth
The correct answer key.
IoU (fit)
Overlap between your box and the true box.
Class
What the object is: car, zebra, healthy leaf.
False alarm
The AI flags something that is not there.
Hallucination
A confident answer that is wrong.

These are teaching simulations. Outcomes come from simple rules, not real AI models or measured safety results.
