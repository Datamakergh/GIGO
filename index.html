How is my box scored?

The fit score says how well your box sits on the object. 100% is exact; 0% means not touching. Too big, too small or shifted lowers it.

  • Under 30%: the AI does not see the object.
  • 30% to 75%: the AI sees it, but late or unsure, and you score less.
  • 75% or more: the AI sees it reliably.
  • 80% or more: full points for that box.
