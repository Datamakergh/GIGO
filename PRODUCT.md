# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Trainees in the SYNAPSE AI Data Annotation Training Programme, often in a classroom or self-paced session on a laptop. They box 10 objects in a road scene, run a simulated self-driving car, and learn from the outcome. Many are new to annotation; some sessions are projected for a group.

## Product Purpose
Teach "Garbage In, Garbage Out": annotation quality directly drives AI behaviour. Success is a trainee who makes a rushed attempt, sees the consequence, corrects their boxes, and sees the improvement in "Before and after".

## Brand Personality
Professional, credible, clear. A calm, confident training tool. Motion is purposeful (car physics, detection cues, result reveals), never flashy or gamified. Art is clean illustrated: flat vector with soft shading and depth, always readable for annotation.

## Anti-references
- Arcade/gamified UIs: confetti, bouncy motion, cartoon exaggeration.
- Photoreal clutter that makes object edges ambiguous to box.
- Implying a real AI model or measured safety result (it is a rule-based simulation).

## Design Principles
1. Legibility of the task first: every object must have clear, unambiguous edges to box.
2. Show consequence, don't tell: the drive itself should make the cause visible.
3. Honest simulation: always signal that the outcome is simulated.
4. Calm confidence: motion explains state changes; it never decorates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Detection states never rely on colour alone (dash patterns, labels). Honour prefers-reduced-motion (no shake, no pulsing, instant transitions).
